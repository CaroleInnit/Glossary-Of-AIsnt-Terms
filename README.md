# The Ultimate AIsn't Governance Dictionary-In-Training

---

**Agentic** (v.) — to create a dangerous adlibber of data, replace all your staff with it, thereby driving off your client base through being unable to deliver even basic first-line service.

**AI governance** (n.) — policies about things that haven't deployed, things that nobody has realised are deployed, and things that are planned for deployment that won't get deployed because the thing that nobody's governing will have already eaten all the data (should be part of overall governance anyway).

**AI governance copy** (n.) — a shout into a void created by a crowd of self-defined experts with no experience.

**AIsn't** (n.) — what we currently call AI but isn't. Nobody can agree on the 'I'. Or the 'A', actually.

**AI Scaling** (n.) — exponential fault and risk amplification, a result of not understanding what they were in the first place.

**Alignment** (n.) — the process of teaching AI human values without understanding that those values do not cohere. see also training data, guardrailing, wrong trousers of time, pluriverse.

**Anthropic** (v.) — to build a trust-and-safety based LLM, and lose goodwill and users through unanticipated success. see AI scaling and tokenisation.

**Bias-audit** (v.) — to discover exactly the problem you were looking for while missing the fifteen that you weren't.

**Black box** (n.) — a system nobody understands, surrounded by people who sell and implement it. Anything AI, basically.

**ChatGPT** (v.) — to ask a precise question and get a precise answer that has very little bearing on context, or reality, or anything really.

**Clauding** (v.) — also pp. clauded. To get excited and ambitious with your projects only to have the token rod pulled from beneath you. ext. overclauding, (pp.) overclauded — to leave a superior LLM because they've pissed you off.

**Codexify** (v.) — to start using an LLM coding app as contingency, only to swap to using it as primary because you've been clauded too much. see clauding, overclauding.

**Copilot** (v.) — to be helped so enthusiastically in the wrong direction that your work takes five times longer than had you done it with Excel. Also, why are you using Copilot?

**DeeCee** (v.) — to use an exceptional researcher that is unable to discuss Tiananmen Square, Taiwan, or anything that happened between 1949 and now. orig. Deep Seek, a world-leading AIsn't.

**Digital twin** (n.) — another copy of something we don't understand.

**Disaster consulting** (n.) — with AI??? You're kidding me.

**Edge case** (n.) — any AI or agentic encounter with real life.

**Explainability** (n.) — post-hoc rationalisation or cop-out.

**Foundation model** (n.) — a building with no planning permission. Or foundations.

**Gemini** (v.) — see Copilot.

**GIGO** (acron.) — an effective aphorism that, while still true, was dumped in the pre-AI era in favour of training data that does exactly that.

**GreenPT** (n.) — a little known, probably-slightly-more-environmentally-friendly AI that is as opaque, sycophantic and obfuscating as all the others.

**Grok** (v.) — to obtain the most biased possible response to a genuine question. And make Heinlein spin in his grave.

**Guardrail** (v.) — to build a fence around the wrong field.

**Ibid** (acron.) — I'm incensed by AI papp that promotes more AI papp.

**LLM** (v.) — to create extraordinary outputs only to discover the only value is the time you wasted on them, because you missed all the sycophancy.

**Meta AI** (v.) — see Gemini, only more so.

**Midjourney** (v.) — to get exactly what you asked for, and realise you asked for the wrong thing and can't articulate what you wanted.

**Multi-modal** (adj.) — with the capacity to get things wrong in several different formats, simultaneously and inconsistently.

**Mythos** (v.) — to build something so dangerous you won't let anyone use it, then accidentally leave the blog post about it in an unsecured public data cache. ext. re-mythos (v.) — then discover you lost control of it anyway.

**OpenAI** (v.) — to open-source your mission statement and close-source everything else.

**Perplexify** (v.) — to get excitingly accurate research results only to discover you've accidentally given away your entire IP.

**Prompt engineer** (v.) — to be absolutely certain of what you're doing, yet surprised that the same prompt gets 15 different responses.

**Prompt injection** (n.) — demonstration that the fence around the wrong field also has inbuilt gaps.

**RAG** (acron.) — correct data paraphrased until the meaning is excised. Once "red amber green", now "really anyone's guess".

**Responsible AI** (n.) — right…

**Surface** (n.) — a thing that describes itself and obfuscates the obvious. (v.) — to make obvious something that actually already was.

**Tokenisation** (n.) — making a token or superficial effort at the consistent quantification of inconsistent charging.

**Training data** (n.) — lowest common denominator papp without context, subtext or thought as to results.
